# Phase 7A — Important Dates for Our Circles

## Purpose

Add a lightweight shared record of dates that matter to a two-person Circle without turning the relationship space into a general productivity calendar.

## Product boundary

Important Dates may preserve:

- Anniversaries
- Birthdays
- Trips
- Recurring traditions
- Other meaningful dates

They do not create:

- Affection streaks
- Relationship-health scores
- Required check-ins
- Automatic emotional judgments
- A general task or work calendar

## Access and consent

- Important Dates are available only while the preserved two-person Circle is unlocked.
- Either equal member may create, edit, or remove a date.
- Locking the Circle hides the dates without deleting them.
- Reopening the same Circle restores the same history.
- Dates never appear on either person’s public profile.

## Recurrence

A date may be:

- One-time
- Repeated yearly

Yearly dates roll forward in the client to their next occurrence. One-time dates remain visible under Past dates after they pass.

## Privacy-safe analytics

The feature records only controlled actions, date category, and whether the date repeats yearly. It does not record titles, notes, calendar dates, conversation IDs, user IDs, or either person’s identity.

## Feature flag

`two_person_circle_important_dates`

## Pass condition

Both members can preserve, edit, and remove meaningful dates in an unlocked Our Circle; yearly dates surface their next occurrence; past one-time dates remain shared history; and locking the Circle removes access without deleting the records.
