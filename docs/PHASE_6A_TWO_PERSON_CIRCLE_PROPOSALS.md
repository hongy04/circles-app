# Phase 6A — Two-person Circle proposal and consent flow

## Purpose

Add the explicit consent step between Mutual Focus and a shared two-person Circle.
Mutual Focus is eligibility, not permission. Creating the Circle requires a new
proposal and a direct response.

## Lifecycle

```text
Mutual Focus active and revealed
→ either person may propose
→ recipient chooses Create our Circle / Not yet / End Focus
```

### Create our Circle

- Activates the existing direct conversation as a two-person Circle.
- Preserves the complete direct-message history.
- Starts the shared Circle Timeline at activation time; old direct-chat media is
  not silently republished into shared history.
- Keeps Mutual Focus active.

### Not yet

- Keeps Mutual Focus active.
- Does not repeat the proposal automatically.
- Transfers the next proposal right to the person who chose Not yet.
- Prevents the original proposer from asking again unless the right is later
  transferred back by another Not yet response.

### End Focus

- Declines the proposal.
- Resets the pair's romantic state.
- Preserves the ordinary accepted connection and direct messages.
- Keeps outside romantic discovery paused until each person resumes it.

## Privacy and pressure boundaries

- Proposal state is visible only to the two people in the focused pair.
- No proposal reminders or public status are created.
- Analytics contain only controlled action names.
- Accepted two-person Circles persist independently of later Focus state.
- Pre-activation direct media is excluded from the Circle Timeline.

## Bounded scope

This phase activates a basic shared Circle profile with Posts and a
post-activation Timeline. Equal profile editing and the two-person Plans flow are
reserved for the next bounded phase.
