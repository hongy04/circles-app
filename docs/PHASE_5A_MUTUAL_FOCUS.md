# Phase 5A — Mutual Focus

## Purpose

Add the deliberate step after Mutual Interest where two accepted connections may independently choose to focus on each other. Focus is not inferred from messaging, time, or engagement. It becomes active only after two private, current selections.

## State progression

```text
Accepted connection
→ reciprocal romantic channel
→ private interest
→ Mutual Interest revealed to each person
→ private Focus selection
→ Mutual Focus revealed to each person
```

## Private selection

A person may choose **Focus on this connection** only after they have personally received the Mutual Interest reveal. Their choice is readable only by them. The other person is not notified unless they independently make the same choice.

## Mutual activation

When both people choose Focus:

- one Mutual Focus state is created;
- romantic discovery with every other connection pauses for both people;
- hearts disappear on outside connected profiles;
- unmatched one-sided romantic selections involving either focused person are cleared;
- ordinary friendships, profiles, messages, events, and Circles remain unchanged;
- each person receives the Focus reveal separately on the next opening of the existing direct conversation;
- only one active Focus connection is allowed per person.

Existing Mutual Interest states with other people remain private and unavailable while discovery is paused. They are not used for ranking, notifications, or recommendations.

## Ending Focus

Either person may end Focus. This:

- removes the Focus state and both Focus selections;
- clears the romantic interest and Mutual Interest state between the pair;
- returns the pair to an ordinary accepted connection;
- preserves their messages and ordinary connection;
- keeps outside romantic discovery paused for both people.

Each person must independently choose **Resume romantic discovery** from Romantic settings before their saved audience becomes active again.

## Privacy rules

- No one-sided Focus choice can be queried by the other person.
- Focus is available only after Mutual Interest and the viewer's reveal.
- Focus cannot exist without an accepted connection and reciprocal romantic channel.
- Setting changes, visibility closure, or connection removal clear incompatible Focus state.
- Analytics contain only controlled action names, never pair IDs, target IDs, conversation IDs, names, or audience details.

## Deferred

The two-person Circle proposal is intentionally deferred to Phase 6. Mutual Focus establishes eligibility but does not create a shared Circle automatically.
